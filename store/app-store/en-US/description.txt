Thirty seconds a day. One line about today, and what you wrote on this exact date in years past.

Purl is not a journaling exercise. It does not ask for paragraphs, prompts or discipline. You write one line, and the app hands you back what you wrote on the same day last year, and the year before that.

REREAD, NOT JUST WRITE
Below today's line, every past entry for this same date, most recent first. The first year there is nothing yet, so Purl shows what you wrote a week ago and a month ago instead, and the exact date this page starts having memories.

ANY DAY, WRITTEN LATE
Missed yesterday, or last month? Write it from the year grid or from search. Purl never blocks a late entry, and a streak only counts days written on time.

A YEAR ON ONE SCREEN
Twelve months, one grid, a filled square for every day you wrote. No red squares anywhere: an empty day is a gap, not a failure. Search finds any word across every year you have written.

LOCKED, FOR FREE
Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, off by default. A private diary should not cost extra to protect: the lock is free, same as the reminder, the widget and the year grid.

ONE REMINDER, AND IT KNOWS WHEN TO STOP
A single daily nudge at the time you choose. It stays quiet the moment you have already written today.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no sign-up, no cloud of ours. Export your whole diary as one file, readable text included, whenever you like, and import it back on another device. The backup is free and always will be: it is the first thing this category charges for, and the last thing we would.

A PHOTO WHEN A DAY NEEDS ONE
Attach a photo to any entry. The first three are free.

FREE, WITHOUT A TIMER RUNNING
Writing, reading every past year, the grid, search, the streak, the reminder, the lock, the widget, and exporting and importing your data: all free, permanently.

PRO, ONE PAYMENT
A photo on every entry, extra cover colors, and the year and lock-screen widgets. One payment, once, never a subscription.

WHAT YOU WILL NOT FIND HERE
No AI summarizing your days, no mental health claims, no streak that punishes a late entry, no ads, no analytics.

Purl works on iPhone and iPad, portrait and landscape.
